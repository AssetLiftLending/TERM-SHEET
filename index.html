How this is calculated Cash to close = Purchase Price − Purchase Loan + Fees. The loan is capped by the lowest of: (1) leverage × amounts, (2) Max LTV × ARV, (3) Max LTC × total project cost. When capped, interest reserves are reduced first, then the purchase loan is reduced. Rehab always remains at 100%.
